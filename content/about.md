# About

Howard Ignatius is a retired high-tech marketing executive from Morro Bay, CA. He grew up in Minnesota surrounded by the natural beauty of Lake Superior and the great “Northland”. Upon graduation from the University of Minnesota, he enlisted in the US Navy and purchased his first 35mm camera and a complete darkroom. When he returned home from the service, the demands of his career took precedence over his photography, and he soon found himself in the fast paced world of Semiconductor Manufacturing in Silicon Valley. As the digital revolution eclipsed film and older imaging technologies, he started to invest in new cameras and software. However, photography would remain a hobby until his retirement. He now combines his love for photography with a strong technology background to compliment his art in non-traditional and unique ways. He has traveled the world extensively and displays his work at www.howardignatius.com and www.nocturnography.com .
